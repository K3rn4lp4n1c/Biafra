The General is a 70-year-old veteran who fought for the side that favored the secession. He is famously irritable whenever the war comes up in conversations and discussions, but he never tires of talking about fishing. He was very reluctant in giving us the information we were looking for. After some negotiation, we struck a bargain with him: a rare specialty fishing hook in exchange for valuable firsthand information about the war that has never been formally recorded. Our intel traced recent sales of the hook to a shady marketplace, along with the program used to access it. Unfortunately, the marketplace was upgraded recently, and the artifact can no longer communicate with it properly but we know the flag should be part of the details of our hook. Use the artifact to get that flag!
