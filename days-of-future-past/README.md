While sifting through the remnants of a compromised historical archive, we recover a black-and-white [picture](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/K3rn4lp4n1c/Biafra/refs/heads/main/days-of-future-past/days-of-future-past.jpg) of vessel personnels. The women in this photo worked at a shipyard that later built a Royal Navy fast patrol boat remembered for its role in a civil war. Trace the vessel back to its original service and identify its original Royal Navy pennant number.
